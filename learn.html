Learn Number Systems

Four short lessons. Read them in order, then try the converter.

1 What is binary?

Binary is a base-2 system with only two digits, 0 and 1. Each digit is called a bit. Computers store everything, from numbers to photos, as patterns of bits.

2 Place values

Every position has a weight that doubles as you move left. To convert to decimal, add the weights of the bits that are 1.

Bit76543210
Weight1286432168421

3 Worked example

01100100 has 1s at weights 64, 32 and 4.

64 + 32 + 4 = 100

In octal this is 144 and in hexadecimal it is 0x64.

4 Octal and hex

Octal is base 8 (digits 0 to 7). Hexadecimal is base 16 (0 to 9 and A to F). Group bits in threes for octal and in fours for hex.

  • 1111 = hex F
  • 111 = octal 7
